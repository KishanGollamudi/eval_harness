# DevGear Store Policies

## Shipping & Delivery

- **Free standard shipping** applies to orders over **$75 USD** before taxes and discounts.
- **Standard delivery:** typically 3–7 business days after dispatch.
- **Express delivery:** typically 1–2 business days after dispatch; the express fee is shown at checkout.
- Delivery estimates are business-day estimates and may change due to carrier or weather delays.
- We currently ship only to addresses in the United States and Canada. International destinations outside these countries are not supported.

## Returns & Refunds

- Eligible items may be returned within **30 days of delivery** in their original condition and packaging.
- Opened audio gear, including headphones and headsets, is subject to a **15% restocking fee**.
- Clearance items are **final sale and non-returnable**, except where required by law.
- After an approved return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Original shipping charges are not refundable.

## Warranty & Support

- Hardware includes a **standard 12-month limited warranty** against manufacturing defects, unless a longer product-specific warranty is listed.
- The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modifications, or normal wear and tear.
- To request support, contact the store with your order number and a description of the issue.
- If troubleshooting does not resolve the issue, support will issue an RMA number and return instructions. Items sent without an approved RMA may be refused.

## Promo Codes & Discounts

- **FDE15** is active and gives **15% off orders over $100 USD** before taxes and shipping. Enter the code at checkout; it cannot be combined with other offers.
- Promo codes are subject to their stated eligibility and expiration dates.
- **Expired promo codes are rejected** and cannot be applied retroactively to completed orders.
